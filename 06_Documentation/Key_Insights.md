# Key Insights

## Executive Summary

The marketing campaign analysis covers 6,240 cleaned campaign records across 24 campaigns, 8 marketing channels, and 5 geographic regions from January 2025 through June 2026.

Overall performance was strong:

- Total Spend: $2.57M
- Total Revenue: $18.22M
- Total Profit: $15.65M
- CTR: 2.37%
- Conversion Rate: 5.60%
- Cost per Conversion: $19.32
- ROAS: 7.09
- ROI: 609.17%

## Channel Performance

- Email was the most efficient marketing channel, generating the highest ROI and ROAS.
- Google Ads generated strong revenue and conversion volume, making it an important scale channel despite lower efficiency than Email.
- Meta Ads and Influencer campaigns also produced strong positive returns.
- YouTube Ads was the least efficient channel overall, with the lowest ROI among the major channels, although it remained profitable.

## Campaign Performance

- Win Back Customers was the strongest campaign by ROI and was identified as a strong candidate for additional budget.
- Lifecycle Nurture and Monthly Product Digest were also among the top-performing campaigns.
- Executive Thought Leadership and Programmatic Market Reach produced negative ROI and should be reviewed, reduced, or paused until performance improves.

## Audience Insights

- Returning Customers generated the highest revenue and conversion volume.
- Enterprise Decision Makers produced the highest ROI.
- Small Business Owners also represented a strong high-value segment.
- Students & Young Professionals showed weaker efficiency and higher acquisition costs, indicating an opportunity to improve targeting and messaging.
- Records classified as Unknown should be treated as a data-quality category rather than a strategic audience segment.

## Regional Insights

- North America generated the highest total revenue and conversion volume.
- Latin America achieved the highest ROI, indicating strong efficiency but lower scale.
- Asia-Pacific also showed strong ROI and represents a potential budget expansion opportunity.
- North America should retain strong investment while focusing on reducing cost per conversion.

## Device Insights

- Mobile generated the highest revenue and conversion volume.
- Desktop delivered the highest efficiency and lower cost per conversion.
- Tablet produced the weakest performance of the three device categories and should be reviewed for optimization.

## Time-Based Insights

- April 2025 generated the highest monthly revenue.
- November 2025 generated the highest conversion volume.
- March 2025 showed the strongest month-over-month revenue growth.
- May 2025 experienced a significant revenue decline compared with the previous month.
- June 2026 showed weaker ROAS than earlier periods and should be monitored.

## Budget Optimization Recommendations

1. Increase investment gradually in high-efficiency channels such as Email while monitoring whether performance remains stable at higher spend levels.
2. Maintain strong investment in Google Ads and Mobile because of their contribution to revenue and conversion volume.
3. Expand high-performing audience segments such as Returning Customers, Enterprise Decision Makers, and Small Business Owners.
4. Review and reduce budget for campaigns with negative ROI, especially Executive Thought Leadership and Programmatic Market Reach.
5. Improve targeting, messaging, and creative strategy for lower-performing audience and device segments.
6. Protect high-volume markets such as North America while focusing on lowering acquisition costs.
7. Test controlled budget increases in Latin America and Asia-Pacific because of their strong efficiency.
